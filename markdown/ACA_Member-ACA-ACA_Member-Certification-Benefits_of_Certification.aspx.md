## Benefits of Certification

**What advantages does Certification offer?**  
ACA designed the Corrections Certification Program to:

- Increase the overall knowledge level of practitioners
- Foster professional development and lifelong learning
- Promote corrections as a rewarding career
- Enhance society’s image of corrections personnel and the field of corrections

**How would becoming a Certified Corrections Professional (CCP) better my staff, my institution, and/or my agency?**  
Becoming a Certified Corrections Professional (CCP) enables staff at all levels to become part of a nationwide network of resources, ideas, opinions, skills, and people that can enhance the productivity, efficiency, and effectiveness of your agency.
