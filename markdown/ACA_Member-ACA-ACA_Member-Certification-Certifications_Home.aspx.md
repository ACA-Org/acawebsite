# Correctional Certification

The Corrections Certification Program (CCP) is a self-study program that gives individuals an opportunity to receive recognition for mastering specialized knowledge and skills.

---

Thank you for trusting the American Correctional Association with your professional development needs.

**We have an important announcement…**

We are redesigning our corrections certification programs, and we are preparing to launch a more streamlined, member-friendly certification process. The new process is expected to be launched in early 2025.

The new process will include:

- Meaningful and customizable certification options
- Affordable certification with member-friendly pricing
- Modernized application submission and processing
- Simplified exam administration
- Reduced turnaround time for certification

During the redesign phase, we have paused the processing of new non-healthcare certification applications. We are pausing acceptance of all CCO, CCS, CCM, and CCE applications.

**NOTE:**  We are still processing, and will continue to process, recertification applications for all credentials **AND** healthcare certification applications.

If you have a question about a **_non-healthcare_** application, please contact:

(703) 224-0195

For healthcare certification applications, please contact the Office of Correctional Health via telephone at (703) 224-0049 or email at [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection).

Thank you for choosing to learn, grow, and excel with ACA!

We appreciate your patience as we diligently work to better serve your certification needs.
