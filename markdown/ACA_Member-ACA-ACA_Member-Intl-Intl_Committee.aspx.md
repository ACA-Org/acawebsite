# ACA’s Committee on International Corrections

The Committee on International Corrections is responsible for promoting the dissemination and exchange of information among corrections practitioners throughout the world. It advises the Executive Committee concerning actions that ACA may initiate to further international communications, subject to the charges from the President and the Board of Governors approval.

## Members of the committee are appointed by the ACA president.
