##  Affiliate with Representation 

Affiliate with Representation: A national or international organization or association representing a major professional discipline engaged in corrections or a related field and that has an interest in the governance of the association and has the ability to vote as a member of the delegate assembly. These affiliates comply with qualifications outlined in their contract.
