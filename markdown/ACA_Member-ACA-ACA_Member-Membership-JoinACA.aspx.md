**Professional I (Domestic)**

Available for those working in corrections or law enforcement. A 1-year domestic membership is **$35** and a 3-year is **$99**.

[Join Now](#)

- [1 year - $35](https://aca.org/ACA/ACA_iCore/Membership/ACA-Join-Wizards/joinindividual.aspx?iProductCode=Professional_Billing_Cycle)
- [3 years - $99](https://aca.org/ACA/ACA_iCore/Membership/ACA-Join-Wizards/joinindividual.aspx?iProductCode=Professional_Billing_3yr)

![](/images/web%20elements/icon-pro1.jpg)

**Professional I (International)**

Available for those working in corrections or law enforcement. A 1-year international membership is **$75** and a 3-year is **$215**.

[Join Now](#)

- [1 year - $75](https://aca.org/ACA/ACA_iCore/Membership/ACA-Join-Wizards/joinindividual.aspx?iProductCode=Professional_1_Intl_1Yr)
- [3 years - $215](https://aca.org/ACA/ACA_iCore/Membership/ACA-Join-Wizards/joinindividual.aspx?iProductCode=Professional_1_Intl_3Yr)

![](/images/web%20elements/icon-assoc.jpg)

**Associate**

(Student) - Available for full-time students who are not currently employed in corrections or law enforcement.

(Retired) - Available for those who have retired from employment in corrections or law enforcement. A 1-year membership is **$25**.

[Join Now](https://aca.org/ACA/ACA_iCore/Membership/ACA-Join-Wizards/joinindividual.aspx?iProductCode=Associate_Billing_Cycle)

![](/images/web%20elements/icon-org.jpg)

**Organizational (Domestic)**

Available for nonprofit entities. A 1-year domestic membership is **$300**.

[Join Now](https://aca.org/ACA/ACA_iCore/Membership/ACA-Join-Wizards/joinorganization1.aspx?iProductCode=Organizational_Billing_Cycle)

![](/images/web%20elements/icon-org.jpg)

**Organizational (International)**

Available for nonprofit entities. A 1-year international membership is **$390**.

[Join Now](https://aca.org/ACA/ACA_iCore/Membership/ACA-Join-Wizards/joinorganization1.aspx?iProductCode=Organizational_Intl)

![](/images/web%20elements/icon-org.jpg)

**Supporting Patron (Domestic)**  
Available to individual or corporate companies providing products and services to corrections or law enforcement, OR available to individual or corporate companies with an interest in the corrections or law enforcement field. A 1-year domestic membership is **$350**.

[Join Now](https://aca.org/ACA/ACA_iCore/Membership/ACA-Join-Wizards/joinorganization1.aspx?iProductCode=Supporting_Patron_Domestic)

![](/images/web%20elements/icon-org.jpg)

**Supporting Patron (International)**  
Available to individual or corporate companies providing products and services to corrections or law enforcement, OR available to individual or corporate companies with an interest in the corrections or law enforcement field. A 1-year international membership is **$440**.

[Join Now](https://aca.org/ACA/ACA_iCore/Membership/ACA-Join-Wizards/joinorganization1.aspx?iProductCode=Supporting_Patron_Intl)
