### **Ask Us How to be a Trainer!**

The ACA is currently accepting applications for correctional trainers from all over the country to lead in our face-to-face trainings.

We are looking for:

- Corrections Professionals who provide training for managers, staff, and/or volunteers of a correctional agency
- Adjunct or field trainers and faculty of institutions of higher learning in a variety of areas regarding corrections, criminal and juvenile justice

This is a great opportunity to share your wealth of knowledge and experience with others in the corrections field.

Submit letter of interest, application, copy of certification, and recommendation letter from a supervisor or colleague.

[Click here to download application](/common/Uploaded files/Professional Development/Correctional Trainer Application Template PDF.pdf)
