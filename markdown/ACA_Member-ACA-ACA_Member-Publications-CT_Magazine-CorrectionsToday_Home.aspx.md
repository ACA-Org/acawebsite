## ACA Celebrates Another Award-Winning Year

Once again, the American Correctional Association (ACA) has been recognized as an award-winning publisher:

- 2020: APEX (Awards for Publication Excellence) excellence in feature writing for "Johnny Cash at Folsom Prison" (_Corrections Today_, Jan/Feb 2019).
- 2018: Azbees (American Society of Business Publication Editors) award for excellence in redesign for _Corrections Today_.
- 2017: Association Media & Publishing Bronze Excel Award for feature article "From Incarcerated Mother to Community Role Model (_Corrections Today_, Jan/Feb 2016).
- 2017: APEX (Awards for Publication Excellence) excellence in feature writing for "Planting Purpose" (_Corrections Today_, Nov/Dec 2016).
- 2016: APEX (Awards for Publication Excellence) excellence in feature writing "ACEs High" (_Corrections Today_, Sep/Oct 2015).
- 2015: APEX excellence in magazine issue for Jan/Feb 2015 _Corrections Today_
- 2015: APEX excellence in books for _Preventing and Managing Riots and Disturbances: Using the Incident Command System for Corrections_.
- 2014: APEX excellence in books for _Cage Your Rage: An Inmate’s Guide to Anger Control, Second Edition_.
- 2013: APEX excellence in magazine issue for April/May 2012 Reentry Programs issue of _Corrections Today_.
- 2013: APEX excellence in books and eBooks for _Pathways for Offender Reentry: An ACA Reader_.
- 2012: APEX excellence in books and eBooks for _An ACA Reader: Jails_.

We want to know what is happening in your world.
