# Publications Department

The Communications and Publications Department comprises book publishing, periodicals, social media and other electronic media, graphics and production. The department plans, produces, promotes and coordinates the publishing efforts of the American Correctional Association.

Our award-winning publications include **_Corrections Today_** magazine; **_Corrections Connect_**, our once-a-month newsletter for ACA members and **_Correction Digest_** a thrice weekly compendium of top correctional news from around the world. 

ACA's social media channels provide a way for ACA members and others interested in correctional issues to stay up-to-date with the latest happenings in ACA and the wider correctional field.

You can also learn how to submit your original article, column or book-length manuscript to ACA for possible publication.

[Email Us](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#fd8d889f91949e9c899492938ebd9c9e9cd3928f9a)
