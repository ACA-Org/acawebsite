- ## How do I write for ACA?

  First, you have to determine whether you want to write an article for *Corrections Today*, or write a book. Please see the link for [*Corrections Today* submission guidelines](http://www.aca.org/aca_prod_imis/ACA_Member/Publications/Corrections_Today_Magazine/Submission_Guidelines/ACA_Member/Publications/CT_Magazine/CT_SubmissionGuidelines_home.aspx). For book publishing see the link on this site or contact publications at [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#99e9ecfbf5f0faf8edf0f6f7ead9f8faf8b7f6ebfe).

- ## I would like to read some of the book manuscripts submitted to ACA and have a hand in determining which ones are chosen to be published.

  Please contact Publications at [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#e19194838d88828095888e8f92a1808280cf8e9386) and she will send you a form to complete about your background and experience. Then, when we have a manuscript in your area of expertise, we will call you to see if you have the time to review the material. If you do, we will send it to you along with an evaluation form to complete. Incidentally, manuscripts and reviews are sent out blind, that is without any of the author's or reviewer's names, so that the author or reviewer can be free of bias or preconceived ideas about the material.

- ## We had some changes in our agency and information in an ACA directory is now wrong. How do we get it corrected?

  To send updated information, contact Publications at [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#9eeeebfcf2f7fdffeaf7f1f0eddefffdffb0f1ecf9) or fax the changes to (703) 224-0000.

- ## How do I advertise a job opening?

  [Please click this link for details.](http://www.aca.org/ACA_Prod_IMIS/ACA_Member/Job_Bank/ACA_Member/Job_Bank/JobBank_Home.aspx)

- ## How can I purchase article reprints from *Corrections Today*, *Corrections Compendium*, or *On the Line*?

  To purchase professionally printed, color or black-and-white article reprints, please submit a written request starting the reason, quantity needed and whether you want color or black-and-white copies. Prices will based upon your specifications. Prepayment by check or credit card is required. For more information, contact publications at 703-224-0000 or [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#29595c4b45404a485d4046475a69484a4807465b4e).

- ## How can I purchase photographs from ACA?

  ACA has a variety of photographs in its photo archives that are available for purchase. There is a research and handling fee of $25 per photo, in addition to duplication charges. ACA will provide the photo credit that must be listed with the photograph. Please specify if you would like to receive prints or electronic copies of the photos. Prepayment by check or credit card is required. Fax or mail your request, specifying how and what the photos will be used for, to: The American Correctional Association, Attn: Photo Research, Publications Department, 206 North Washington Street, Suite 200, Alexandria, Virginia 22314; fax 703-224-0000. For more information, contact publications at [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#d0a0a5b2bcb9b3b1a4b9bfbea390b1b3b1febfa2b7).

- ## How do I place orders, inquire about orders, or place a standing order?

  To Place an Order:   
  Prepayment of orders is required. ACA accepts Visa, MasterCard, American Express and Diner's Club credit cards as well as checks/money orders and institutional/agency purchase orders. All orders are shipped UPS ground service unless express service is requested. Shipping and handling charges are added to all orders. P.O. Box addresses are not accepted for shipping.

- [Click here to download the Product Catalog Order Form.](/aca_prod_imis/Docs/aca_orderform.pdf) 

  **EMAIL/PHONE:  
  **To order by email, contact: [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#0566766077736c6660456466642b6a7762) and by phone, call us toll-free at 1-800-222-5646. We need your credit card number, expiration date, the cardholder's name as it appears on the card and the billing address along with your order information: name and item number of the product you are ordering, number of copies of each item, and shipping address. Our hours of operation are from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. EST, Monday through Friday.

  **FAX/MAIL:  
  **

  You are welcome to fax an ACA order form with credit card payment information or a valid institutional/agency purchase order form to 240-396-2473. Order forms and check/money order payments may be mailed to: ACA, Customer Service Department, 206 N. Washington St., Suite 200, Alexandria, Virginia 22314. Please do not fax and mail your order, or send a duplicate copy for confirmation. This may cause a duplication of your order. You may call our customer service department at 1-800-222-5646 to confirm receipt of an order. 

  To Inquire About an Order:  
  Please contact customer service by email at [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#c0a3b3a5b2b6a9a3a580a1a3a1eeafb2a7). Call us at 1-800-222-5646 and for local calls and those outside the United States, dial 703-224-0000. 

  To Place a Standing Order:  
  ACA's four directories and standards supplements are available for standing-order service. If you wish to be placed on a standing-order for these publications as they are printed, please send a letter stating the title or titles you wish, and a request to be placed on the standing-order list. Include your billing and shipping addresses. You will begin receiving the most current edition. Confirmation letters and payment requests are mailed to standing-order customers approximately six weeks before new titles are issued.

- ## Can I receive discounts on ACA publications?

  ACA members receive a 20 percent discount on ACA published materials. Professional I, II and Executive Gold members receive this discount when placing orders and paying by credit card or check/money order. When using an institutional/agency purchase order, you must have an Organizational or Supporting Patron membership to receive this discount. Please see [Membership](http://www.aca.org/aca_prod_imis/ACA_Member/Membership/ACA_Member/Membership/Membership_Home.aspx) for more information on discounts and benefits. 

  In addition to membership discounts, customers can receive discounts by ordering in bulk. When you purchase 25 or more copies of an ACA book, course, directory or standards manual, you may be entitled to additional discounts. Discounts range from 25 percent to 45 percent off of the nonmember price. Contact a customer service representative at 1-800-222-5646, ext. 0197, (email: [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#2241514750544b4147624341430c4d5045)) to learn more.

- ## If I have a book or video that is not published by ACA, is it still possible to have it in the ACA catalog?

  Yes, it is. We distribute a number of books and videos from other publishers/producers. We are always looking for valuable additions to our catalog. If you have something that you think would be of interest to our members, please contact Roberta Gibson, customer service manager, at 1-800-222-5646, ext. 0198; [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#ef9d88868d9c8081af8e8c8ec1809d88), to find out what you will need to do to have your materials considered.
