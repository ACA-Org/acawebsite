FAQs

You may renew by contacting the Membership Services Team at (703) 224-0115 for assistance.

You may contact our Membership Services Team at (703) 224-0115 to join ACA. Alternatively, you may visit our website and complete the online membership application form.

Your membership will begin immediately after we have received your application and payment. 

The American Correctional Association accepts major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, or American Express), debit cards and checks for membership payments.  

We currently offer the following membership types: 
 
Professional – available for those working in corrections or law enforcement.

Associate (Student) – available for full-time students who are not currently employed in corrections or law enforcement.

Associate (Retired) – available for those who have retired from employment in corrections or law enforcement.

Organizational – available for nonprofit entities.

Supporting Patron – available to individual or corporate companies providing products and services to corrections or law enforcement, OR available to individual or corporate companies with an interest in the corrections or law enforcement field.

If you are a new member, your membership ID number will be included on your receipt. You will receive a welcome letter (electronic or mailed) confirming your membership. The letter will contain your membership number in the upper right hand corner. You may also contact our Membership Services Team at (703) 224-0115 for assistance with obtaining your Member ID number.

We no longer distribute membership cards. The Membership Services Team will email your Certificate of Membership with your Welcome or Renewal letter. You may also receive it upon request by calling the Membership Services Team at (703) 224-0115.

To cancel your membership, please contact the Membership Services Team at (703) 224-0115. A team member will be happy to assist you.
